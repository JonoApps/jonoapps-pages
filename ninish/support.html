Do I need an account?

No — the app works entirely offline with no sign-in required.

Does this app require the AI feature to work?

No. It comes with dozens of built-in responses. The OpenAI API key field is completely optional.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you choose to add your own OpenAI API key.
