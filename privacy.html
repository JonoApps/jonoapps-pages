Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Ninish Feelings, Allegedly ("the app") is designed with your privacy in mind.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. All mood entries, streak history, and settings are stored locally on your device, and shared only with the app's own widget through a private iOS App Group so your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can display your streak.

Optional AI feature

The app includes an optional field where you may enter your own OpenAI API key. If you choose to add a key, the text you type into the app is sent directly from your device to OpenAI's API to generate a personalized response. This is entirely optional, is initiated only by you, and uses your own OpenAI account and API key — we do not operate any server, and no data passes through us. If you don't add a key, the app uses built-in pre-written responses and never sends any data anywhere.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party, except as described above (OpenAI, and only if you supply your own API key).

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
